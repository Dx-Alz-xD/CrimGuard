What Red records about how you work

Red scores how each account behaves, to catch data being taken out of it. That means it records things about you, and you should be able to read exactly what. Everything below is visible to you about yourself, from the Risk button in the bottom-left corner of any signed-in page.

RecordedNot recorded
How fast you type, in averages over a minute: the gap between keystrokes and how long keys are held. Which keys you press. No key identity ever leaves the page, and nothing you type is sent or stored.
How fast the pointer moves and how you scroll, again as averages. Where the pointer went. No positions, paths or clicks.
That you copied or pasted, how many characters, and whether it matched the shape of a password or an API key. What you copied. The text is checked inside the page and thrown away; only the category name ("api_key") is sent.
Which projects and pages you opened, when, and what you searched for in the people directory. What is inside your files. The risk database records that a file was opened or downloaded and how large it was, never its contents.
Sign-ins, failures, the browser you used, and a coarse fingerprint of the device. Your precise location. Red stores the network your address belongs to and your browser's time zone, nothing finer.
When you print a page, take a screenshot or start screen sharing, and how many Red tabs you have open. Anything outside Red. A web page cannot see your other applications, files or devices, and this one does not try.

Who can see it

You can see all of it about yourself. Admins and the CEO can see everyone's scores and the variables behind them, which is how someone taking data out of Red would be noticed. In CrimGuard they also see each person's projects, files, devices and activity: admins can open files up to Restricted, the CEO every file. Opening someone's record there is itself logged. The risk database keeps counts and timings, never the contents of your work.

Turning it off

Red works normally without the risk database attached: unset CRIMGUARD_DATABASE_URL and remove database/crimguard.db, and nothing is recorded or scored. The behaviour of every other page is identical.

Red Runs on Node.js with a built-in SQLite database.
